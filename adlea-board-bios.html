Director

Dr. Ani Gemalmazian

Associate Director of Student Services • USC Marshall School of Business

Dr. Ani Gemalmazian is a higher education leader with a decade of experience in student services and higher education administration. She serves as Associate Director of Student Services at the University of Southern California’s Marshall School of Business, where she oversees key components of student life from admission through post-graduation, cultivating supportive environments for student leaders and delivering impactful co-curricular programming. She advises the Business Analytics Student Association, leads the Student Ambassador Program, and directs the Orientation Advisors team. Across all of this work, her mission is the same: to empower underrepresented youth, guide them through their academic journeys, and help them realize their full potential.

As a scholar-practitioner, Ani focuses on higher education leadership and educational equity. Her research examines how intersectionality shapes the leadership experiences of Armenian-American women in higher education.

Ani’s commitment to Armenian language learning grows out of that identity research and a lifelong dedication to sharing knowledge. It began during her undergraduate years at UC Berkeley, where she served as a teaching assistant for beginner Armenian courses and was selected by the Berkeley Language Center to develop Armenian language learning content for its digital platform. During the COVID-19 pandemic, she designed and taught virtual Armenian courses through Hye Tutors, expanding access to heritage language instruction for learners far beyond the classroom.

Ani is drawn to ADLEA by its mission and the community of educators behind it. She is especially eager to contribute to ADLEA’s work in Professional Growth, Advocacy and Consulting, Translation and Interpreting, and Parent Education, bringing her experience in leadership development, research, and community engagement to the service of educators, students, and families.

Ani holds a B.A. in Psychology from UC Berkeley, a Master of Public Administration from the USC Sol Price School of Public Policy, and an Ed.D. in Educational Leadership from the USC Rossier School of Education.

ADLEA is a nonpartisan organization. We do not represent, endorse, or advance the political agenda of any party, government, or institution. Our work is rooted in something older and more enduring: the wisdom, depth, and continuity carried by Armenian culture and language across generations. We bring that inheritance into conversation with modern pedagogy, neuroscience, and human-centered education to prepare a generation of learners ready for the world ahead. This is the whole of our mandate: nothing more, nothing less.
