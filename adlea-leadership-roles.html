Strategic Leadership Position

ADLEA Wellbeing Guide

ADLEA's Wellbeing Guide leads the integration of holistic wellbeing into the heart of Armenian DLI programs, guiding whole-child, whole-teacher approaches grounded in neuroscience, trauma-informed practice, and ancestral wisdom. The role spans whole-school wellbeing integration, developing frameworks that support the emotional, mental, and cultural needs of learners and educators while training school teams in social-emotional learning, self-regulation, and mind-body practices, alongside preventative mental health supports for learners navigating identity, migration, and multiliteracy demands. The Wellbeing Guide champions educator wellbeing and capacity-building through resources, peer support groups, and wellness coaching that foster resilience, work-life harmony, and collective care, working closely with instructional leaders to align wellbeing with pedagogy and classroom design. A central thread of this role is cultural healing and identity development, infusing Armenian storytelling, music, and ritual into school culture, supporting identity-affirming spaces, and helping design programs that address intergenerational trauma and diaspora belonging. The Wellbeing Guide also evaluates the impact of wellbeing initiatives on learner engagement, teacher retention, and school climate, co-creating sustainable models of support that can be adapted across global contexts, and serves as a thought leader in reimagining Armenian dual language education as a source of healing, growth, and thriving for the entire community.

ADLEA Technology & Digital Strategy Guide

ADLEA's Technology & Digital Strategy Guide architects, manages, and continuously evolves the digital ecosystem that powers ADLEA's global mission, ensuring the online platform, digital media, and technology tools remain seamless, strategic, and scalable. The role centers on digital ecosystem management, building and maintaining LEHZU, ADLEA's open-access digital platform, alongside the website and future app, while monitoring and troubleshooting the systems that connect departments across the organization. It also encompasses digital media and content infrastructure, coordinating livestreams, webinars, and professional learning events, and managing the storage, editing, and presentation of ADLEA's growing video library of instructional best practices. The Technology & Digital Strategy Guide leads technology integration and innovation, guiding thoughtful use of AI, automation, and digital solutions that enhance the experience of educators and users while identifying emerging tools that strengthen ADLEA's digital reach. In partnership with the Partnership and Outreach Guide, the role shapes ADLEA's branding and online presence, overseeing social media strategy and ensuring visual and digital consistency across every public-facing channel. Finally, the Technology & Digital Strategy Guide safeguards cybersecurity and data ethics, ensuring data protection and safe access across ADLEA's digital platforms while upholding accessibility, privacy, and transparency in every technology decision.

ADLEA Professional Growth Guide

ADLEA's Professional Growth Guide oversees the design and delivery of professional learning experiences that inspire excellence in dual language teaching, facilitating powerful collaborations, cultivating teacher leadership, and guiding instructional practice that fosters biliteracy, biculturalism, and deep content understanding. The role centers on learning leadership and educator development, designing and delivering professional learning focused on DLI pedagogy, language acquisition, and content integration across languages, while facilitating communities of practice and maintaining a robust training calendar of workshops, virtual coaching, and expert-led series. It also encompasses pedagogical innovation and instructional design, co-creating model units and instructional tools grounded in research-based practices in biliteracy and translanguaging, and supporting educators in integrating content, language objectives, and cultural competence. The Professional Growth Guide drives global collaboration and capacity building, sustaining networks of Armenian DLI educators through online platforms and cross-site learning exchanges, engaging field experts and master teachers to enrich the professional learning ecosystem, and coaching instructional leads on effective mentorship models. Finally, the role ensures quality assurance and strategic planning, aligning all professional learning to ADLEA's vision and global benchmarks for bilingual education, tracking professional growth goals, and contributing to the development of ADLEA's professional learning standards and frameworks.

ADLEA Partnership and Outreach Guide

ADLEA's Partnership and Outreach Guide leads relationship-building and strategic communication efforts that shape ADLEA's presence and impact on a global scale, building and nurturing partnerships with school districts, universities, diaspora organizations, government agencies, and cultural institutions. The role centers on outreach and engagement, serving as ADLEA's external representative in public forums, media opportunities, conferences, and community events, and crafting clear, compelling messages that communicate ADLEA's mission to diverse audiences. It also encompasses strategic communication, building visibility and influence across digital platforms and real-world networks, developing tailored communication strategies, and creating public-facing materials that articulate ADLEA's mission, programs, and accomplishments. The Partnership and Outreach Guide drives partnership development, proposing, negotiating, and co-designing collaborative projects that advance ADLEA's goals while maintaining strong connections with diaspora communities and Armenian institutions globally. The role brings innovation and resourcefulness to outreach, proposing creative and strategic collaborations and leveraging technology to track partnership metrics and engagement impact, while supporting program development by gathering insights from partners to help shape the direction of ADLEA's projects and initiatives, including assistance in securing grants, sponsorships, and collaborative research opportunities. Finally, the Partnership and Outreach Guide contributes to ADLEA's strategic direction and vision, embodying and communicating the organization's core values of unity, innovation, wellbeing, and cultural rootedness.

Strategic Leadership Position

ADLEA Operations Guide

ADLEA's Operations Guide architects and manages the systems, structures, and logistics that bring ADLEA's programs to life, ensuring that every initiative reflects excellence and equity in practice as the grounding force behind ADLEA's day-to-day and long-term operational vision. The role centers on program implementation and support, overseeing the logistics that sustain Armenian DLI program delivery across school sites, partnering with district leaders on scheduling and staffing models, and advising on enrollment strategy to balance native and non-native speakers with integrity and inclusion. It also encompasses staffing and professional capacity, supporting districts in recruiting and onboarding qualified bilingual and Armenian-speaking educators while facilitating collaborative systems between educators, schools, and ADLEA's learning guides. The Operations Guide ensures curriculum, assessment, and instructional support by aligning Armenian and partner language curriculum resources with standards and best practices and contributing strategic input on instructional models across grade levels. The role drives community and stakeholder engagement, building strong relationships with school and district partners, families, and the broader Armenian-speaking community while ensuring clear, bilingual communication across every outreach channel. It includes budgeting and resource management, supporting the Finance and Strategy team in managing operational budgets and grant allocations while ensuring efficient procurement of instructional materials and technology, alongside compliance, monitoring, and reporting, tracking program adherence to regulatory requirements and supporting evaluation processes that ensure continued quality and impact. Finally, the Operations Guide contributes to strategic planning and growth, shaping ADLEA's long-term vision for expanding Armenian DLI programs globally and serving as a thought partner in designing future initiatives.

ADLEA Learning Guide

ADLEA's Learning Guide serves as the anchor of instructional excellence, delivering model biliteracy lessons in Armenian and one or more partner languages, creating a vibrant repository of video-based best practices, and designing transformative professional development for DLI educators globally. The role centers on instructional leadership, designing and delivering high-quality, standards-based biliteracy lessons across elementary grade levels, recording and annotating exemplar lessons to build a rich video library, and modeling effective strategies for language development, academic rigor, and cultural relevance while providing coaching and mentorship to teachers implementing DLI instruction. It also encompasses curriculum and professional learning, co-creating culturally grounded, interdisciplinary curriculum resources in Armenian and a partner language, developing and facilitating professional development sessions and toolkits on biliteracy and instructional design, and collaborating with educators and scholars to integrate neuroscience, social-emotional learning, and global competencies into curriculum development. The Learning Guide also contributes to the translation, adaptation, and creation of academic resources for DLI classrooms. Finally, the role champions collaboration and innovation, partnering with local and global teams to align teaching strategies with evolving research and community needs, innovating across virtual and in-person learning settings, and serving as a visible instructional leader who embodies curiosity, care, and cultural pride.

ADLEA Finance, Growth & Strategy Guide

ADLEA's Finance, Growth and Strategy Guide leads the development of a sustainable, mission-aligned financial ecosystem that powers the global impact of Armenian Dual Language Immersion education, overseeing grants, fundraising, donations, membership, and platform revenue while ensuring transparent reporting and compliance aligned with nonprofit best practices. The role centers on strategic financial leadership, designing ADLEA's financial systems, models, and growth strategy while creating annual budgets, forecasts, and cash flow projections aligned with programmatic goals. It also encompasses fundraising, grants, and donations, researching and writing grant proposals for private foundations and global institutions, cultivating relationships with donors and philanthropic partners, and leading fundraising campaigns and donor pathways that support ADLEA's mission. The Finance, Growth and Strategy Guide drives revenue and resource development, managing ADLEA's membership structure and platform sales, exploring new funding channels such as sponsorships and co-branded partnerships, and supporting the pricing and scalability of ADLEA's professional learning offerings and resources. The role ensures financial operations and reporting, managing nonprofit financial reporting, compliance, and audits while overseeing bookkeeping and maintaining fiscal integrity and transparency across the organization. Finally, the Finance, Growth and Strategy Guide contributes to organizational growth and strategic insight, identifying and forecasting long-term opportunities for mission-aligned expansion and collaborating with other Guides to align fiscal decisions with impact and sustainability.

ADLEA Research and Data Guide

ADLEA's Research and Data Guide leads the organization's first full-scale research agenda into Armenian Dual Language Immersion, designing studies, building data systems, and translating findings into practice that strengthens every other Guide's work. The role centers on research design and implementation, leading original studies on program outcomes, biliteracy development, and learner identity and wellbeing, developing research questions and methodologies in partnership with ADLEA's leadership and field experts, and conducting longitudinal and comparative studies across ADLEA's global network of programs. It also encompasses data infrastructure and systems, building and maintaining ADLEA's data collection systems across programs, assessments, and platforms while designing dashboards and reporting tools that make data usable for Guides, educators, and district partners, all grounded in data privacy, security, and ethical use. The Research and Data Guide drives assessment and program evaluation, collaborating with the Professional Growth Guide and Learning Guide to align assessment tools with research priorities and providing data-driven recommendations that inform curriculum, professional learning, and program design. Finally, the role brings strategic insight and advocacy, translating research findings into clear narratives that support ADLEA's advocacy and partnership efforts, identifying trends across the Armenian DLI field, and serving as ADLEA's thought leader on research and evidence-based practice in dual language education.

Strategic Leadership Position

ADLEA Student Voice Guide

ADLEA's Student Voice Guide brings the lived experience of Armenian Dual Language Immersion directly into the organization's leadership, ensuring that the perspective of the students the program serves shapes ADLEA's decisions, advocacy, and public voice. The role centers on lived-experience advocacy, sharing firsthand insight into how Armenian DLI shapes identity, language, and belonging, and helping ADLEA's other Guides understand the real impact of programs, policies, and resources on the learners who move through them. It also encompasses peer and community outreach, connecting with current and prospective DLI students and their families to answer questions, demystify the program, and encourage a new generation of learners and advocates, while supporting efforts aimed at expanding DLI into new districts and communities. The Student Voice Guide contributes to program and policy conversations, offering a learner's perspective in discussions about curriculum, assessment, and program design, and helping ensure that decisions made on behalf of students are grounded in what students themselves experience and value. Finally, the role serves as a public representative of ADLEA's mission, participating in advocacy efforts, community events, and storytelling initiatives that illustrate, in the clearest possible terms, why Armenian Dual Language Immersion matters.
