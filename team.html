Strategic Leadership Positions

Dr. Alina Vehuni

Founder, Keeper of the Vision

Dr. Alina Vehuni is a visionary educator, consultant, and coach with over 20 years of impact in multilingual education and systemic transformation. As former Teacher Specialist for the Armenian FLAG program in Glendale Unified, she led its evolution into a high-performing dual immersion model through curriculum design, educator training, and community building. Today, as a consultant for LAUSD, she continues shaping the future of Armenian Dual Language Immersion.

Dr. Vehuni champions dual language education as a catalyst for global competence and cognitive advantage. Through ADLEA, she is building a platform to unify Armenian dual language educators and elevate the next generation of bilingual, biliterate global citizens.

Dr. Stella Gukasyan

ADLEA Research and Data Guide

Dr. Stella Gukasyan is a transformational healthcare and education leader with over 20 years of experience advancing organizational performance, program innovation, and systems transformation. Her work spans healthcare, government, academia, and community-based organizations, where she has led complex federally and state-funded initiatives, multidisciplinary teams, and strategic partnerships.

Specialist Teams

Focused Program Support Roles

Barbara Ter-Papyan

Teaching and Learning Specialist

With degrees in Pedagogy and Linguistics and more than 40 years of teaching experience, Barbara Ter-Papyan has dedicated her career to Armenian language education, including as a founder of Ararat Charter School and a National Board-certified educator. She joined LAUSD's Dual Language Immersion Program in 2019, bringing decades of expertise in Armenian language and culture directly into the DLI classroom.

Hasmik Chobanyan

Translation and Interpreting Specialist

Hasmik Chobanyan holds a Master's degree in Armenian Language and Literature and has taught in Glendale Unified School District's Armenian Dual Language Immersion Program since 2009, extending her work in Armenian DLI since 2019 to Los Angeles City College. She has contributed to curriculum development that strengthens DLI and is dedicated to helping her students develop both linguistic proficiency and a deep appreciation for Armenian culture.

ADLEA is a nonpartisan organization. We do not represent, endorse, or advance the political agenda of any party, government, or institution. Our work is rooted in something older and more enduring: the wisdom, depth, and continuity carried by Armenian culture and language across generations. We bring that inheritance into conversation with modern pedagogy, neuroscience, and human-centered education to prepare a generation of learners ready for the world ahead. This is the whole of our mandate, nothing more, nothing less.
