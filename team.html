ADLEA’s Organizational Structure

A Teal Organization

ADLEA is organized as a Teal organization, a model in which people lead through trust, shared purpose, and self-management rather than a top-down chain of command. That is why our leaders are called Guides.

Self-Management

Lead Guides and Specialist Teams take ownership of their areas of work and make decisions close to where the work happens, guided by the mission and by the people their decisions affect.

Wholeness

Educators, leaders, and partners bring their whole selves to ADLEA, including their languages, cultures, identities, and lived experience. Wellbeing is part of how we work, not separate from it.

Evolutionary Purpose

ADLEA’s mission is a living purpose that grows with the communities we serve. Our Founder serves as Keeper of the Vision, listening for where that purpose is calling us next.

ADLEA is a nonpartisan organization. We do not represent, endorse, or advance the political agenda of any party, government, or institution. Our work is rooted in something older and more enduring: the wisdom, depth, and continuity carried by Armenian culture and language across generations. We bring that inheritance into conversation with modern pedagogy, neuroscience, and human-centered education to prepare a generation of learners ready for the world ahead. This is the whole of our mandate: nothing more, nothing less.
