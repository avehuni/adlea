About ADLEA

The first organization solely dedicated to Armenian Dual Language educators and communities worldwide.

Our Vision

The Foundation We Build From

Youth Empowerment

Inspiring future-ready leaders through meaningful education

We nurture confident, creative, and multilingual young people who are ready to lead with empathy, curiosity, and purpose in an interconnected world.

Inclusive Ecosystem & Collaborative Innovation

Fostering a culture of equity, shared growth, and collective brilliance

ADLEA brings together passionate educators, communities, and institutions to co-create and share ideas, tools, and practices. We cultivate spaces where inclusion, innovation, and cultural knowledge flourish together.

Cultural Roots, Global Reach

Armenian heritage wisdom as educational bedrock for embracing the world

Our foundation is built on the wisdom and richness of Armenian culture. From these deep roots, we connect across continents to inspire globally-minded, culturally-aware education for all.

Our Mission

How We Bring the Vision to Life

Elevate Educators as Changemakers

We invest in people who make transformation possible - the educators. Through dynamic, culturally grounded training, we unlock their full potential as visionary leaders and community builders.

Design Curriculum That Honors the Past and Builds the Future

We fuse Armenian cultural wisdom and legacy with forward-thinking pedagogy to create learning that is relevant, identity-affirming, and globally resonant.

Transform Learning Through Brain-Based Practices

We embed the science of how young minds grow into every facet of dual language education, creating classrooms where learning is joyful, trauma-informed, and deeply human.

Amplify Armenian Voices on the Global Stage

We advocate for the Armenian language worldwide, expanding its reach, relevance, and power in a multilingual, multicultural future.

ADLEA believes that when educators are empowered, cultural wisdom is celebrated, and innovation is shared, we raise a generation that is ready to lead with wisdom, voice, and purpose.
