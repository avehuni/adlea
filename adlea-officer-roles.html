Corporate Officer

President

Currently held by Dr. Alina Vehuni

The President is an officer of ADLEA, Inc. and serves as its chief executive officer. The President is responsible for the day-to-day management of the Corporation’s operations, staff, and programs, subject to the direction and oversight of the Board of Directors, and holds the powers and duties the Board prescribes by resolution or written delegation of authority.

When the Board authorizes it, the President establishes the staff leadership positions and reporting structures needed to carry out ADLEA’s programs and operations, such as ADLEA’s Lead Guides and Specialist Teams, and may define the work of advisory bodies under Board-delegated authority. The President may also call a special meeting of the Board.

Because the President is compensated by ADLEA, the President does not chair the Board. The President’s compensation is reviewed and approved in advance as just and reasonable by directors who are not interested persons, and the President is absent from any deliberation and vote on it. Dr. Alina Vehuni also holds the permanent honorary title of Founder of ADLEA.

Responsibilities: Chief executive officer · Day-to-day management of operations, staff, and programs · Powers and duties set by the Board · Staff leadership structure when authorized · May call special Board meetings

Bylaws Sections 4.3, 4.11, 4.16, 4.17, 5.1, 5.8, 6.2, 6.3

Vice President

Currently held by Dr. Mariné Avagyan

The Vice President is an officer of ADLEA, Inc. who works alongside the President in leading the Corporation’s operations, staff, and programs, and carries out the responsibilities the Board of Directors assigns to the office.

The Vice President helps translate the Board’s direction and ADLEA’s strategic priorities into action, leads and coordinates the initiatives and areas of work entrusted to the office, and supports the President in guiding the staff leadership team. The Vice President attends Board meetings, reports to the Board on the work under the office’s responsibility, and represents ADLEA with partners and the community when asked to do so.

The Vice President also helps ensure continuity of leadership, carrying out the President’s responsibilities when the Board so directs, and performs any other duties the Board assigns.

Responsibilities: Supports the President in leading operations, staff, and programs · Leads initiatives assigned by the Board · Reports to the Board · Represents ADLEA when asked · Continuity of leadership

Bylaws Section 5.1

Treasurer

Currently held by Dr. Stella Gukasyan

The Treasurer serves as the Chief Financial Officer of ADLEA, Inc. The Treasurer oversees the Corporation’s financial affairs and ensures that accurate financial books and records are kept.

The Treasurer ensures that a report on the Corporation’s financial condition is presented at each regular Board meeting and at any other time the Board requests, and renders an accounting of the Corporation’s transactions and financial condition whenever the Board asks.

Under the bylaws, the Treasurer may not also serve as Chair of the Board or President, and the Treasurer and Secretary must be different people. The Treasurer performs any other duties the Board assigns.

Responsibilities: Chief Financial Officer · Oversight of financial affairs · Accurate books and records · Financial report at each regular Board meeting · Accountings on request of the Board

Bylaws Sections 4.16, 5.1, 5.7

Corporate Officer

Secretary

Currently held by Luiza Cortezian

The Secretary ensures that minutes of all Board and committee meetings are taken and maintained, and that all notices are duly given as required by the bylaws and by law.

The Secretary is the custodian of ADLEA’s corporate records, including the Articles of Incorporation and Bylaws kept at the Corporation’s principal office, receives written resignations from directors and officers, and certifies the adoption of the Bylaws on behalf of the Corporation.

Under the bylaws, the Secretary may not also serve as Chair of the Board or President, and the Secretary and Treasurer must be different people. The Secretary performs any other duties the Board assigns.

Responsibilities: Minutes of Board and committee meetings · Notices of meetings · Custodian of corporate records · Receives resignations · Certifies the Bylaws

Bylaws Sections 4.6, 5.1, 5.6, 5.9, 8.4

We bring that inheritance into conversation with modern pedagogy, neuroscience, and human-centered education to prepare a generation of learners ready for the world ahead.
