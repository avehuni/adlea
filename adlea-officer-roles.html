ADLEA Corporate Officers

ADLEA Inc. is a California nonprofit public benefit corporation. Its officers carry the legal and fiduciary responsibilities that keep the organization accountable to its mission and community.

Corporate Officer

President

Currently held by Dr. Alina Vehuni

The President is the chief executive officer of ADLEA Inc. and, subject to the control of the Board of Directors, has general supervision, direction, and control of the corporation’s activities and affairs. The President provides leadership in carrying out ADLEA’s mission and ensures that the policies and decisions of the Board are put into effect.

The President presides at all meetings of the Board of Directors and of the members, sets meeting agendas in consultation with the other officers, and serves as an ex officio member of Board committees as provided in the bylaws. The President represents ADLEA publicly and in its relationships with partners, funders, government agencies, and the communities it serves.

Together with the Secretary or Treasurer, the President may sign contracts, agreements, and other instruments authorized by the Board on behalf of the corporation. The President also reports regularly to the Board on the corporation’s activities, progress, and challenges, and performs any other duties assigned by the Board or the bylaws.

Responsibilities: Chief executive officer · Board and member meetings · Implementation of Board policy · Public representation · Authorized contracts and instruments · Reports to the Board

Corporate Officer

Vice President

Currently held by Dr. Mariné Avagyan

The Vice President supports the President in leading ADLEA Inc. and helps ensure continuity of leadership. In the absence or disability of the President, the Vice President performs all the duties of the President and, when so acting, has all the powers of, and is subject to all the restrictions on, the President.

The Vice President works closely with the President and the other officers on the corporation’s priorities, may chair or oversee Board committees and special initiatives as assigned, and helps prepare the organization’s leadership for future transitions.

The Vice President performs any other duties prescribed by the Board of Directors, the President, or the bylaws.

Responsibilities: Acts for the President when needed · Leadership continuity · Committees and special initiatives · Duties assigned by the Board

Corporate Officer

Treasurer

Currently held by Dr. Stella Gukasyan

The Treasurer is the chief financial officer of ADLEA Inc. The Treasurer keeps, or ensures that the corporation keeps, adequate and correct books and records of accounts of its properties and business transactions, including its assets, liabilities, receipts, disbursements, gains, and losses.

The Treasurer oversees the deposit of all money and other valuables in the name and to the credit of the corporation with the depositories the Board designates, and the disbursement of corporate funds as the Board directs. The Treasurer presents the annual budget and regular financial statements to the Board, and renders an account of all transactions and of the financial condition of the corporation whenever the Board or the President requests.

The Treasurer works with the Board and ADLEA’s accountants to ensure timely filing of required tax returns and reports, including IRS Form 990 and California state filings, and supports the annual review or audit of the corporation’s finances. The Treasurer may also serve on or chair the finance committee and performs any other duties prescribed by the Board or the bylaws.

Responsibilities: Chief financial officer · Books and financial records · Deposits and disbursements · Budget and financial statements · Tax and state filings · Annual review or audit

Corporate Officer

Secretary

Currently held by Luiza Cortezian

The Secretary keeps, or ensures that the corporation keeps, a book of minutes of all meetings, proceedings, and actions of the Board of Directors, its committees, and the members, recording the time and place of each meeting, those present, and the actions taken.

The Secretary keeps a copy of the Articles of Incorporation and the bylaws, as amended, at the corporation’s principal office, and gives, or causes to be given, notice of all meetings of the Board and members as required by the bylaws and California law. The Secretary also maintains the corporate seal, if any, and the official records of the corporation, including any record of members.

The Secretary helps ensure that the corporation’s required filings with the California Secretary of State, such as the Statement of Information, are made on time, and may countersign authorized documents on behalf of the corporation. The Secretary performs any other duties prescribed by the Board or the bylaws.

Responsibilities: Minutes of Board and committee meetings · Articles and bylaws · Notices of meetings · Corporate records · Statement of Information · Countersigning authorized documents
