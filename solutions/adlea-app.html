Expanding Access

ADLEA App

Access to resources, community, and event information through a customized app.

Everything in One Place

The ADLEA App is where the global Armenian DLI community comes together. Whether you are looking for a lesson plan, connecting with a fellow educator across the world, or registering for an upcoming conference, the app brings every dimension of ADLEA's work into a single, beautifully designed space, built specifically for the people doing this work, and accessible from anywhere.

What the App Delivers

Your Entire Armenian DLI World. One App.

One Place for Resources, Community, and Events

Access LEHZU's full library of Armenian DLI instructional resources, connect with the ADLEA educator network, and stay current on professional development opportunities, conferences, and community events, all from one app, on any device.

Built Specifically for Armenian DLI Educators

The ADLEA App is not a generic education platform. Every feature, every resource, and every community space is designed with the specific needs of Armenian DLI educators in mind, wherever in the world their program operates.

Stay Connected to ADLEA from Anywhere

The Armenian DLI community is global and dispersed. The app closes that distance, keeping educators informed, connected, and supported no matter where in the world their program operates.

ADLEA is a nonpartisan organization. We do not represent, endorse, or advance the political agenda of any party, government, or institution. Our work is rooted in something older and more enduring: the wisdom, depth, and continuity carried by Armenian culture and language across generations. We bring that inheritance into conversation with modern pedagogy, neuroscience, and human-centered education to prepare a generation of learners ready for the world ahead. This is the whole of our mandate, nothing more, nothing less.
