Online Resource Platform

LEHZU is an open-source platform where Armenian DLI educators discover, share, and elevate the resources that make their classrooms come alive.

Introducing LEHZU

The Word for Language.
The Home for Everything It Takes to Teach It.

LEHZU, the Armenian word for language, is ADLEA's open-source platform built specifically for the Armenian Dual Language Immersion community. Think of it as a dedicated space modeled on the spirit of Teachers Pay Teachers, designed from the ground up for the educators, schools, and families who are doing the extraordinary work of keeping Armenian alive in classrooms around the world.

LEHZU will offer both free and premium resources, so that every educator, whether in a well-funded district or a small community school, has access to quality materials. Educators who contribute their own work can offer it freely or set their own terms, building a marketplace that honors the time and craft that goes into great teaching.

Every resource on LEHZU will be reviewable by the educators who use it, allowing the community itself to surface what works, refine what needs improving, and build a living library that grows smarter over time.

How It Works

From Discovery to Classroom, in Three Steps

1

Browse and Download

Search LEHZU by grade level, subject, language proficiency, or resource type. Filter by free or premium. Download what you need in minutes.

2

Use in Your Classroom

Bring vetted, community-tested materials directly into your DLI instruction. Every resource is designed with the Armenian bilingual learning context in mind.

3

Review and Contribute

Leave a review to help fellow educators. Upload your own materials to give back to the community and earn recognition for your expertise.

For Educators, By Educators

A Platform That Gives Back to the Hands That Build It

LEHZU is not a passive repository. It is a living, breathing professional community where the educators who use it are the same ones who shape it. Contributors are recognized for their work, reviewers help the best materials rise, and every upload strengthens the collective intelligence of the network.

Whether you are a veteran DLI teacher with years of classroom-tested materials or a new educator looking for a strong foundation, LEHZU is designed to meet you where you are and grow with you.

Upload Your Work

Share lessons, units, and materials you have developed, freely or as premium offerings you set the terms for.

Earn Community Recognition

Contributor profiles, download counts, and peer reviews build your professional reputation within the ADLEA network.

Review What You Use

Your honest feedback helps every educator who comes after you find the right resource faster.
