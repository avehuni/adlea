Lead Guide

Dr. Alina Vehuni

Founder, Keeper of the Vision

Dr. Alina Vehuni is a visionary educator, international speaker, consultant, and coach whose nearly three decades of work in multilingual education and systemic transformation have been shaped by her own experience of holding Armenian heritage and American life within one identity. As former Teacher Specialist for the Armenian FLAG program in Glendale Unified, she led its evolution from inception into a high-performing dual immersion model through curriculum design, educator training, and community building, and today, as a consultant for LAUSD, she continues shaping the future of Armenian Dual Language Immersion.

She holds a Doctorate in Educational Leadership from the USC Rossier School of Education, has served as a Fulbright Specialist in Croatia, and hosts The Potentiality Podcast, where she brings her work into dialogue with experts across fields. She is also the Founder of Wholeness Education, a global humanistic platform reimagining parenting and education around a singular conviction: that cultivating the inner architecture of the future human is the true foundation of learning, growth, and flourishing. She is also the Co-Founder of The Freedom School, a worldschooling education model where teenagers learn how to think rather than what to think, combining a guaranteed academic foundation with self-directed projects, live seminars, and mentorship from a global community.

Dr. Vehuni champions dual language education as a catalyst for global competence and cognitive advantage. Through ADLEA Inc., the Armenian Dual Language Educators Association, she leads a global, grassroots movement built by educators, for educators, to uplift Armenian language, identity, and cultural intelligence through dual language education. The organization brings together educators, families, leaders, and advocates in Armenia and across the diaspora to strengthen Dual Language Immersion programs, elevate both Eastern and Western Armenian to equal footing as standardized literary traditions, and equip the next generation of learners to become bilingual, biliterate global citizens.

Lead Guide

Dr. Stella Gukasyan

ADLEA Research and Data Guide

Dr. Stella Gukasyan is a transformational healthcare and education leader with over 20 years of experience advancing organizational performance, program innovation, and systems transformation. Her work spans across healthcare, government, academia, and community-based organizations, where she has led complex federally and state-funded initiatives, multidisciplinary teams, and strategic partnerships. Through implementation science, program evaluation, and evidence-based leadership, she translates research and innovation into sustainable organizational and population-level impact.

Dr. Gukasyan champions collaborative leadership as a catalyst for organizational excellence and measurable impact. Her expertise includes primary care transformation, population health, behavioral health integration, infectious diseases prevention, quality improvement, workforce development, and health equity. She is committed to building high-performing systems, strengthening cross-sector partnerships, and developing the leaders and infrastructure needed to translate evidence into lasting change. She brings this same commitment to evidence-based leadership and systems thinking to her role as ADLEA's Research and Data Guide, helping translate what the field learns into practices that strengthen Armenian Dual Language Immersion Programs for educators and learners alike.

Lead Guide

Dr. Regine Muradian

ADLEA Wellbeing Guide

Dr. Regine Muradian is a licensed clinical psychologist, author, speaker, and expert TV commentator. In her clinical practice, Dr. Muradian works with children, adolescents, and adults presenting with a wide range of emotional stressors. She specializes in anxiety, ADHD, depression, executive functioning, and leadership mental health training, and is a Certified Clinical Anxiety Treatment Professional (CCATP). She is fluent in multiple languages and incorporates her multicultural background into her practice and trainings.

Dr. Muradian has led mental health trainings nationally and internationally for many organizations, training employees on mental health wellness in France, Greece, Morocco, and London. She is a nationally and internationally sought-after media expert on a wide variety of topics and has been featured by NBC, ABC, Cheddar, Fox, MSN, Huffington Post, Nike, HLN, and CBS, among others.

She received her undergraduate degree in International Relations/Business Marketing from the University of Southern California, followed by a master's degree and a doctorate in Clinical Psychology. She completed her training at Cedars-Sinai, Children's Hospital, and the Glen Roberts Child Study Center.

Lead Guide

Lia Harutyunyan

ADLEA Student Voice Guide

Lia Harutyunyan is a strong advocate for the Armenian Dual Language Immersion programs and an aspiring leader who loves Armenian language, culture, and education. Having participated in the Armenian DLI personally, she brings first-hand knowledge and an authentic learner perspective to ADLEA's mission of creating programs that help youth develop their language skills and cultural identity.

As Student Voice Guide at ADLEA, Lia aims to make sure that learner perspectives are included in discussions about Armenian Dual Language Immersion. She works with learners and families in community outreach to increase opportunities for the next generation to learn the Armenian language in the immersion model.
